## 2. What LifeOS does

LifeOS is a personal goal- and habit-tracking app. Core functionality:

- An AI-driven onboarding conversation that proposes a personalized plan,
  schedule, and set of habit/behavior trackers, which you review and can
  edit or reject before anything is created (see Section 4 on AI content).
- Daily logging against your trackers, including structured and free-text
  reasons when you miss a target.
- AI-generated daily check-ins (and, in the future, weekly reviews) that
  read your recent activity and suggest adjustments, which you accept, edit,
  or reject — LifeOS never silently changes your plan or trackers on your
  behalf.
