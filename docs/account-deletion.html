Delete Your LifeOS Account

How to permanently delete your LifeOS account and data.

Option 1: Delete in the app

  1. Open the LifeOS app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

This is immediate and permanent — there is no recovery period.

Option 2: Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, or can't sign in, email [contact email] from the email address associated with your account and ask us to delete it. We will verify the request and delete your account manually.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes your account and every piece of data associated with it, including:

  • Your profile (email, display name, timezone)
  • Your plans, goals, schedule blocks, tasks, and habits
  • Your trackers and every logged tracker entry, including notes and miss reasons
  • Your onboarding chat transcript and any AI-generated proposals from it
  • Your AI check-in and weekly review history (narratives, suggested diffs, and your decisions on them)
  • Your subscription/trial status record

This is a real deletion, not a deactivation — your account row and every table row referencing it are removed. There is currently no grace period or "undo" once you confirm deletion in the app or a deletion request is processed. See the Privacy Policy, Section 8, for what happens to data before deletion is requested (data retention).

How long it takes

In-app deletion is immediate. A deletion request sent by email is processed manually and may take a few days .
